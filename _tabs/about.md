Hi! I'm **Davide Guerri**.

I am a cybersecurity professional, vulnerability researcher, penetration tester, and university professor teaching ethical hacking and binary exploitation. Previously, I led the Vulnerability Management Team at Facebook (Meta).

This blog is where I share technical deep-dives, exploit write-ups, vulnerability research notes, and random engineering projects across:

- **Binary Exploitation & Reverse Engineering**: Heap exploitation, stack overflows, CTF challenges, and tooling (GDB, Ghidra, pwntools).
- **Embedded & Hardware Security**: Firmware analysis, TPM 2.0, cryptographic primitives, and IoT/router research.
- **AI & Machine Learning in Security**: Using NLP and embeddings for vulnerability classification (CVSS prediction, threat intelligence).
- **Systems & Cloud Engineering**: Infrastructure as Code, Linux internals, Go, and resilient architectures.
